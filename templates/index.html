SMART NUTRITION

Fuel your training

Simple principles instead of complicated diet rules.

🥩

Protein

Prioritize every meal

For regular resistance training, a common range is around 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day, adjusted to your needs.

🍚

Carbohydrates

Fuel your performance

Rice, oats, potatoes, fruit and other carbohydrate foods can support training energy and recovery.

🥑

Healthy fats

Keep meals balanced

Include nuts, seeds, eggs, fish, avocado or other suitable sources in sensible portions.

💧

Hydration

Drink consistently

Needs vary with heat and sweat. Increase fluids around demanding sessions and hot weather.

⚠️ Important: FitBuddy provides general fitness education, not medical diagnosis or individualized medical treatment.
